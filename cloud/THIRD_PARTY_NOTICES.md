# UI component credits

The microphone switch is adapted from Uiverse.io by **cssbuttons-io**.
The browser-window card is adapted from Uiverse.io by **boryanakrasteva**.
The layered-ring animation CSS is adapted from Uiverse.io by **Nawsome**;
the SVG geometry was recreated from the user's screenshots.
The active liquid-metal loading animation is adapted from Uiverse.io by
**chase2k25**, recoloured cyan and violet for Jarvis.
The round microphone control beside the message field is adapted from Uiverse.io
by **sahilxkhadka**; the real Streamlit recorder supplies the microphone icon.
The purple/pink neon message bar is adapted from Uiverse.io by **Lakshay-art**.
The white icon-rail sidebar and its SVG icons are adapted from Uiverse.io by
**3bdel3ziz-T**. Its controls use Streamlit's real accessible navigation inputs.
The frosted-glass sidebar background and blurred gradient accent are adapted
from Uiverse.io by **monkey_8812**.
